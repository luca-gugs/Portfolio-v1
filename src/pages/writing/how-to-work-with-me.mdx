Hi, I'm Luca. I'm a founding engineer at RetailReady. This is how I work, what I expect from you, and what you can expect from me. If we're about to work together, read it.

## Honesty goes both ways

I'll tell you the truth, including the uncomfortable parts and “I don't know.” I'll also tell you how sure I am. Sometimes I've done the research and sometimes it's a gut call, and you should know which one you're getting.

I push my ideas hard, sometimes harder than they deserve. Don't let me push them all over you. Push back, bring evidence, and fight for yours while I fight for mine. I'll respect the debate, and when you're right I'll change my mind.

From you I want the truth, especially when it's bad news, and feedback that isn't softened for my sake. You'll get mine directly and fast.

## I'll teach you everything I know

Ask me anything, whenever. I'll explain the reasoning behind a decision and stay on it until it clicks. I expect the same back. When you figure something out, teach it to the rest of us.

## It's going to work out

I'm an optimist. When something breaks, my first assumption is that it's fixable and we're going to fix it.

## Read your code

Agents write most of my code now. I still read every line of it, and I expect you to read yours. If your name is on the PR, you should be able to explain all of it. “The agent wrote it” is not an answer in code review.

## I give it everything

I take work home with me and stew on it at night. I expect the people I work with to care that much too.

## I want to keep getting better

I put in time outside work to get better as an engineer and a product thinker. Every 6 to 9 months I want to look back at my old work and think “wow, I used to suck.” If I don't, I'm not growing fast enough. I expect you to cringe at yours too.

## Let's talk

If you disagree with something on this page, tell me. The sooner the better.
