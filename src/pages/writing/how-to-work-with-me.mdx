Hi, I'm Luca. I'm a founding engineer at RetailReady. This is how I work, what I expect from you, and what you can expect from me. If we're about to work together, read it.

## How I work

### Honesty goes both ways

I'll tell you the truth, including the uncomfortable parts and “I don't know.” My opinions are strong but loosely held. If I've done the research, I'll tell you. If it's a gut call, I'll tell you that too.

I push my ideas hard. Don't let me push them all over you. Fight for yours and I'll fight for mine, and I promise I'll respect the debate. When you're right, I'll change my mind. The right answer matters more than whose answer it was.

Don't soften feedback for me either. The faster you tell me what I'm doing wrong, the faster I stop doing it.

### I'll teach you everything I know

I'll explain the why, not just the what, and I'll stick with it until it clicks. I expect the same back. When you figure something out, teach it to the rest of us.

### It's going to work out

I'm an optimist. When something breaks, my first assumption is that it's fixable and we're going to fix it.

### Read your code

Agents write most of my code now, which makes reading it more important, not less. If your name is on the PR, you should have read every line and be able to explain it. “The agent wrote it” is not an answer in code review.

### I give it everything

I take work home with me and stew on it at night. I expect the people I work with to care that much too.

### I want to keep getting better

I put in time outside work to get better as an engineer and a product thinker. Every 6 to 9 months I want to look back at my old work and think “wow, I used to suck.” If I don't, I'm not growing fast enough. I expect you to be doing the same.

## What I expect from you

- Tell me the truth, especially when it's bad news.
- Push back when you think I'm wrong. Bring evidence.
- Teach what you learn.
- Bring the same commitment I do.
- Cringe at your own work from 6 to 9 months ago.

## What you can expect from me

- Direct feedback, fast.
- Transparency into how sure I am of a given take.
- Anything I know, whenever you ask.
- A real change of mind when you win an argument.
- Optimism when things go sideways.
- Everything I have.

## Let's talk

If you disagree with something on this page, tell me. That's kind of the point.
